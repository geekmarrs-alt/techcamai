Common questions

Can I really self-host for free?
Yes. Community is free, no time limit, no licence key. Up to 4 cameras with all core features. There's no catch — we want you to try it.
What counts as a "site"?
A single TECHCAMAI deployment — one Docker Compose stack managing one location's cameras. Multiple buildings on the same LAN count as one site.
Is my footage ever sent to the cloud?
Never. All processing, storage, and playback happens on-device. TECHCAMAI doesn't phone home — not even for licence checks in Community mode.
When is Operator Pro available?
We're in early access. Contact us to get on the list — early access customers get a discounted rate and direct input into the feature roadmap.
